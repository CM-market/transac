## The Primary Idea

# "Verified Locally. Sold Instantly." — a trust-first, WhatsApp-native launch campaign.

### Working title
**"Buy from someone you can trust. Order in a tap."**

---

### One-line pitch
> Transac is the only local marketplace where every device is verified, every store can be
> badged, and every order is one WhatsApp tap away.

---

## Why this idea

Transac has three genuine, defensible differentiators that most marketplace ads ignore.
This campaign puts all three on the front cover:

1. **Device-verified identity (proof-of-work).** Every access is bound to a verified device.
   This is a rare, honest *trust* story in a category full of fake-review anxiety.
2. **WhatsApp ordering.** No app-to-app payment onboarding, no new habits to teach. The
   buyer is already on WhatsApp — the order happens where the conversation already lives.
3. **Verified Store badges + bilingual reach (EN/FR).** Gives credible sellers a status
   symbol to share and lets the campaign run twice (English and French) with local, shareable
   store links.

The angle leads with **trust + zero-friction**, not "another marketplace app." That is the
distinction that makes it a *campaign*, not a feature list.

---

## Core message

**Problem we speak to:** buyers don't trust random online sellers, and sellers struggle to
prove they're real.

**Our promise:** *You know who you're buying from. Ordering takes one tap.*

**Proof points (all exist in the product):**
- Verified device access (POW) before someone can buy or sell.
- "Verified Store" badge sellers can earn.
- Orders flow straight into a WhatsApp chat with the supplier — no payment setup needed.
- Ratings and reviews on product and store pages.
- English + French, so the whole community gets the same trust.

---

## Target audience

- **Primary (buyers):** local shoppers, 18–45, already heavy WhatsApp users, wary of
  scams and anonymous online sellers, want a real human behind the listing.
- **Secondary (sellers):** micro-merchants and informal-store owners who want a
  credibility badge and an easy way to share their store link (WhatsApp / SMS / social).
- **Geographic focus:** communities where WhatsApp is the default communication channel and
  where local, in-language trust matters (matches the EN/FR product).

## Suggested channels

| Channel | Action |
|---|---|
| **In-app onboarding** | Show "Verified device ✓" and "Order via WhatsApp" on the buyer welcome screen (right after role selection). |
| **WhatsApp / SMS** | Seller shareable store link with a built-in tagline: "Verified store. Order in a tap." |
| **Social (Instagram / Facebook / X)** | Short "one trust moment" ads: a buyer tapping order → WhatsApp chat opens with a real local supplier. |
| **Store listings (Google Play / App Store)** | Refresh listing copy + screenshots around the campaign to echo "Verified locally. Sold instantly." (see `store-listing/`). |
| **Community / referrals** | Encourage verified sellers to share their badge; inbound share links do the promotion for us. |

## Why it works (the logic)

- **Differentiated:** competitors advertise *selection* and *price*; we advertise *proof*.
- **Low-friction conversion:** WhatsApp ordering removes the biggest drop-off point in
  marketplace funnels (account + payment onboarding).
- **Self-multiplying:** the Verified Store badge and shareable links turn sellers into the
  marketing channel, not just the users.
- **Local & bilingual:** runs natively in EN and FR without re-skinning.

---

## Risks / watch-outs

- **No hard proof-of-work detail in marketing copy.** The POW mechanic is a great
  engineering trust signal but too technical for consumers — keep it to "device-verified,"
  not "proof-of-work."
- **Subjectivity.** This is a creative deliverable with no hard definition of done; the
  angle, headline, and channels are easily swapped if SENTINEL/leadership prefers a
  different voice (e.g., price-led or community-led).
- **Verification cap.** The "Verified Store" badge must be genuinely attainable, or the
  trust claim collapses; scale the badge application flow alongside any launch push.
